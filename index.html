Questions

Frequently Asked

What is a venture studio?

A venture studio is a company that creates startups internally. Unlike a VC that only invests, or an accelerator that only mentors, we build. We provide the idea, the team, the capital, and the infrastructure — then spin out independent companies.

How is ARIMITA different from a VC or accelerator?

We are the co-founder from day zero. We don't wait for decks. We validate markets, build MVPs, hire early teams, and operate alongside founders until the venture is ready to fly solo.

Can I pitch my existing startup to ARIMITA?

We primarily build from scratch, but we occasionally partner with exceptional founders who have early traction and a clear technical edge. Reach out via our Connect section.

What industries do you focus on?

We focus on four verticals: digital infrastructure, global trade networks, AI-native applications, and consumer technology. We believe these are the highest-leverage areas for the next decade.

How do I invest in ARIMITA or its ventures?

We are currently building our investor network. Join our newsletter to receive the investment thesis and early access to venture decks before public announcement.
